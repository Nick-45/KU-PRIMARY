# Edupriva System Onboarding & User Training

Welcome to Edupriva! This document serves as your quick-start guide to mastering the system.

## 1. Getting Started
- **Login**: Use your school-provided credentials.
- **Dashboard**: Your home hub for real-time stats and alerts.
- **Chat Support**: Need help? Click the **LABAN** icon at the bottom right of the screen.

## 2. Core Navigation
- **Students**: Manage admissions, records, and promotions.
- **Teachers**: Manage staff profiles and access.
- **Exams & Results**: Manage exams, scores, and generate report cards.
- **Fees**: Track collections, invoices, and fee structures.
- **School Profile**: Configure your school's custom subjects and class structures.

## 3. LABAN (Your AI Assistant)
LABAN is trained to help you with navigation and basic tasks.
- **Examples**:
    - "How do I add a new student?"
    - "How much is the termly fee?"
    - "List students in Grade 7."
- **Support**: If LABAN cannot resolve a technical issue, it will connect you with our developer.

## 4. Administrative Tasks
- **Fees**: Manage fee structures and payments.
- **Reports**: Generate analytics and report cards for students and teachers.

For further support, please contact your school administrator.
